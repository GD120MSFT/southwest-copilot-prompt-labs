LONESTAR AIR — MONTHLY VARIANCE MEMO (FICTIONAL PRACTICE DATA)
Period: June 2026        Prepared by: Senior Financial Reporting Analyst
Distribution: CFO, VP Finance, Business Unit Leaders
=======================================================================

1. HEADLINE
Operating expense for June closed at $1,712.4M against a budget of $1,668.0M,
an unfavourable variance of $44.4M (2.7%). Two thirds of the variance sits in
three account lines. Operating margin closed at 5.2%, 60bps below plan.

2. DRIVERS

2.1  Overtime  —  $17.7M unfavourable (+48.6%)
The June irregular-operations event at MDW and DEN drove crew and ground
overtime well beyond plan. Approximately $12.1M is directly attributable to
the event window (11–14 June). The residual $5.6M reflects continued vacancy
coverage in line maintenance, which has now run above plan for four
consecutive months.
Assessment: $12.1M is non-recurring. $5.6M is structural until the maintenance
vacancy position closes.

2.2  Cloud & Hosting  —  $9.7M unfavourable (+24.6%)
The migration of the reservations platform ran two months longer than planned,
so we carried duplicate on-premises and cloud cost through the whole quarter.
Technology forecasts the duplicate run ends in August.
Assessment: timing. Expect reversal in Q4, not Q3.

2.3  Professional Fees  —  $8.4M unfavourable (+31.2%)
Advisory spend on the fleet financing workstream was not budgeted at this
level. $5.9M relates to work that was approved in March but not reflected in
the June forecast.
Assessment: structural for the remainder of the year. Forecast requires update.

3. FAVOURABLE OFFSETS
Jet fuel closed $11.2M favourable on lower into-plane pricing and a 1.8%
reduction in block hours. Distribution closed $3.1M favourable on channel mix.

4. OUTLOOK
July operating expense is expected to remain above plan, primarily on
professional fees and the tail of the cloud duplicate run. We expect to be
back within 1% of plan by September.

•  Confirmation of cloud duplicate-run end date — owner: CIO office — due 8 July
