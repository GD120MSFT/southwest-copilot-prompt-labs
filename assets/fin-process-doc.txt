LONESTAR AIR — MONTH-END CLOSE PROCESS (FICTIONAL PRACTICE DATA)
Financial Reporting  ·  Process document  ·  Last updated 14 March 2026
=======================================================================

OVERVIEW
The close runs over eight business days. Financial Reporting coordinates;
FP&A, Treasury and the business units contribute inputs. The process below
is written as narrative because that is how it was handed over.

THE PROCESS

On the final calendar day of the month the reporting mailbox sends the
cut-off notice to all cost centre owners. Anything received after noon on
business day two is accrued rather than posted, though in practice we have
accepted late items up to business day four when the amount is material and
the owner has called ahead.

Business day one is systems. The Accounting Systems Specialist runs the
subledger close in sequence — AP, then AR, then fixed assets — and confirms
each has closed cleanly before starting the next. If AP fails, everything
behind it waits. There is a check on the interface log which somebody
should do but which is not written down anywhere.

Business day two, the cost centre extracts come out of SAP. These arrive in
whatever format the requester configured, which is a recurring problem —
merged headers and numbers stored as text are normal. Financial Reporting
normalizes them by hand into the standard template. This takes most of a day.

Business day three and four are the journals. Standard recurring journals
post first. Depreciation is entered manually from the asset register rather
than linked, which is a known weakness. The prior-year adjustment, if there
is one, is posted here and requires the VP Finance's email approval, though
that approval is sometimes given verbally and written up afterwards.

Business day five the statements are drafted. The Financial Reporting Senior
Accountant checks the workbook — cross-tab ties, formula integrity, and the
summary against the detail. This check is the last line of defence and is
performed by one person.

Business day six the variance memo is drafted by the Senior Financial
Reporting Analyst, using the prior month as a pattern. It cannot be
completed until FP&A supply driver notes, which they supply when they can.

Business day seven is review. The CFO reviews the memo and the statements.
Comments come back the same day, usually late.

Business day eight the package is issued. Treasury separately confirms cash
and investment positions, which is not really part of this process but
happens in the same window and is often confused with it.

KNOWN ISSUES NOBODY HAS OWNED
  • The interface log check on business day one.
  • Depreciation entered manually.
  • The extract format changing without notice.
  • Verbal approval on the prior-year adjustment.
  • Driver notes arriving late and gating the memo.
  • Everything downstream of the statement integrity check depends on one
    person being available.

SYSTEMS
  SAP (subledgers, extracts) · Anaplan (budget/forecast) · Excel (statements,
  workpapers) · SharePoint (memos, checklists, process docs) · Workiva (SOX
  evidence) · Quickbase (variance commentary distribution)
