SME NOTES — COMPLEX REFUND & COMPENSATION CASES (FICTIONAL PRACTICE DATA)
Compiled by Sr. Rep Support for the Senior Rep development track
=======================================================================

WHEN A SENIOR REP CAN ACT ALONE
  • Involuntary cancellation refund to original form of payment, any amount.
  • Loyalty point restoration up to 25,000 points.
  • Goodwill credit up to $100 where the delay or error is clearly ours.
  • Written acknowledgement of inconsistent information given at a station.

WHEN TO ESCALATE TO A SPECIALIST
  • Goodwill above $100, or any cash (not credit) compensation.
  • Anything where the customer has used the words "attorney", "lawyer",
    "small claims", "DOT complaint", or names a news outlet.
  • Confirmed special-assistance failure (auto moderate risk, POL-ACC-003).
  • Any case where two customers on the same flight are getting different
    outcomes — consistency risk.

THE FIVE POLICIES A SENIOR REP MUST KNOW COLD
  1. POL-REF-004  Involuntary cancellation refunds — and that they are NOT automatic.
  2. POL-REF-011  Alternate transportation — we do not reimburse unless we
                  directed it in writing or a station leader authorized it.
  3. POL-LOY-002  Loyalty restoration — up to 30 days, despite what the FAQ says.
  4. POL-COM-009  Statements at the point of disruption — acknowledge the
                  inconsistency, never name or blame an individual.
  5. POL-ACC-003  Special assistance — confirmed failure is moderate risk minimum.

THE MISTAKES WE SEE MOST
  • Mirroring the customer's escalated language back at them.
  • Writing "we apologize for the error on our part" — that is an admission.
  • Promising a timeframe the refund system cannot meet.
  • Quoting the Rapid Perks FAQ instead of POL-LOY-002.
  • Closing the case without the four required remark elements.
  • Solving the case in front of them and missing the pattern behind it.

SYSTEMS A NEW SENIOR REP NEEDS
  Week 1: case system (read/write), Customer Care SharePoint, the policy
          library, the remarks template, the quality framework.
  Week 2: refund initiation, loyalty restoration, the STATION-COMMS tag.
  Week 3: the risk criteria, the LEGAL-24 queue, cross-team hand-off routing.
  Week 4: calibration participation, case-notes library contribution.

CASE TYPES WORTH SHADOWING
  Involuntary cancellation with a delayed refund · special assistance failure
  · same-flight multi-customer disruption · an attorney demand letter
  · a case where policy and precedent disagree.
