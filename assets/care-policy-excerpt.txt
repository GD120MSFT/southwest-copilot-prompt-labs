POL-REF-004  Involuntary Cancellation Refunds
Version 6.2  —  Effective 12 March 2026  —  Owner: Refunds Policy Council
When LoneStar Air cancels a segment, the customer is entitled to a full refund
to the original form of payment, whether or not the fare was refundable, and
whether or not the customer accepted a rebooking that they subsequently did
not use. Refunds are issued within 7 business days for credit card and 20
business days for all other forms of payment. Loyalty currency debited against
a cancelled segment is restored in the same cycle.
NOTE: Refunds are NOT automatic. They are initiated on request or on detection
by the daily cancellation sweep, whichever occurs first.

POL-REF-011  Alternate Transportation Purchased by the Customer
Version 3.0  —  Effective 4 November 2025
LoneStar Air does not reimburse third-party transportation purchased by the
customer unless (a) it was purchased at LoneStar Air's written direction, or
(b) a station leader authorized it at the time of disruption. Goodwill gestures
outside this rule require Senior Specialist approval and are logged as
exceptions.

POL-LOY-002  Loyalty Currency on Disrupted Travel
Version 2.4  —  Effective 18 January 2026
Rapid Perks points debited for a segment that was not flown due to carrier
cancellation are restored automatically within one accrual cycle (up to 30
days). Manual restoration may be requested after day 30.
KNOWN CONFLICT: The public Rapid Perks FAQ page still states "points are
returned within 7 days". A correction has been requested (ticket KM-2291).

POL-COM-009  Statements at the Point of Disruption
Version 1.8  —  Effective 2 May 2026
Station personnel must not characterize refunds as automatic. Where a customer
was given incorrect information at a station, the response should acknowledge
the inconsistency of the information provided without attributing fault to a
named individual, and the case should be tagged STATION-COMMS for the monthly
station feedback report.
