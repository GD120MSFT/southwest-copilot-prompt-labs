   AFFECTS: Senior Specialists, Sr. Rep Support.
   ACTION: Use the new LEGAL-24 queue, not the general escalation queue.

2026-07-14  |  GUIDANCE  |  Goodwill on processing delays
   Standard goodwill for a refund processing delay is $100. Amounts above
   this require a documented Senior Specialist rationale in the case.
   AFFECTS: All Specialists.
   NOTE: Issued after the June calibration flagged LSA-39440 ($250, no
   rationale) as inconsistent with LSA-38812 ($100).

2026-07-02  |  PROC-DOC-007 v2.1  |  Case remarks format
   Remarks must now record: request, decision, policy basis, amount.
   Free-text-only remarks will fail the quality review.
   AFFECTS: All Reps and Specialists.

2026-06-25  |  POL-ACC-003 v1.4  |  Special assistance failures
   Any case involving a confirmed assistance request that was not met is
   now automatically classified moderate risk minimum.
   AFFECTS: Sr. Rep Support (risk triage).

2026-06-18  |  POL-COM-009 v1.8  |  Statements at the point of disruption
   Adds the STATION-COMMS tag and the required acknowledgement language.
