########################  VERSION A  —  PROC-DOC-007 v2.0  ###############
                              Effective 4 April 2026
                        CASE REMARKS AND DOCUMENTATION

1. PURPOSE
   To ensure every case carries a record of what was done and why.

2. WHEN TO WRITE REMARKS
   Remarks should be written before a case is closed.

3. WHAT TO INCLUDE
   A summary of the interaction in the Representative's own words.
   Where compensation was offered, note the amount.

4. FORMAT
   Free text. No required structure.

5. REVIEW
   Remarks are spot-checked during monthly calibration.

6. RETENTION
   Remarks are retained for 24 months.


########################  VERSION B  —  PROC-DOC-007 v2.1  ###############
                              Effective 2 July 2026
                        CASE REMARKS AND DOCUMENTATION

1. PURPOSE
   To ensure every case carries a defensible record of what was requested,
   what was decided, and the basis for that decision.

2. WHEN TO WRITE REMARKS
   Remarks MUST be written before a case is closed. A case cannot be
   closed in the system without a remarks entry.

3. WHAT TO INCLUDE  —  ALL FOUR ELEMENTS ARE REQUIRED
   (a) What the customer requested
   (b) What was decided
   (c) The policy basis, cited by policy number
   (d) The amount, where any refund, credit or goodwill was issued
   Where a decision departs from the standard outcome, the rationale must
   be recorded and, above $100 goodwill, attributed to a named Senior
   Specialist.

4. FORMAT
   The standard remark template. Free-text-only remarks will fail the
   quality review and be returned to the author.

5. REVIEW
   Remarks are reviewed on every case sampled for quality, not only at
   calibration. Failure rate is reported to team leads weekly.

6. RETENTION
   Remarks are retained for 24 months.

7. RELATED
   See POL-COM-009 for language requirements where information given at a
   station was inconsistent with policy.
